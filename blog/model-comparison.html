Model Showdown: v3 vs v4 vs Stock YOLOv8n

Published January 24, 2026 | By Artifactiq ML Team

Executive Summary

We ran a comprehensive comparison of three YOLOv8n models on our merged_all_datasets validation set (7,570 images). The goal: determine which model performs best for our 39-class detection taxonomy.

Winner: v3-epoch1 leads with mAP50 of 1.63% - over 7x better than stock YOLOv8n (0.22%) and significantly ahead of v4-epoch1-safe (0.003%).

CLI Test Results

We tested both Artifactiq models using the CLI on the same sample image:

$ artifactiq analyze --input coco_val_000001.jpg --model v4-epoch1-safe --confidence 0.01
Detected 0 artifacts (29ms)

$ artifactiq analyze --input coco_val_000001.jpg --model artifactiq:v3.0.1 --confidence 0.01
Detected 8 artifacts (28ms)
v4 detects 0 objects while v3 detects 8 on the same image. This confirms v4 needs significantly more training epochs.

Analysis

Why v3 Wins

  • More training iterations: v3-epoch1 had more training cycles leading to better weight convergence
  • Balanced dataset: 18K images with better class distribution
  • Higher recall: 22.85% vs 1.12% for stock - finds 20x more relevant objects

Why Stock YOLOv8n Underperforms

  • Different taxonomy: Trained on COCO's 80 classes, not our 39-class schema
  • No domain knowledge: Cannot detect apparel, accessories, or fashion items
  • Class mismatch: Only ~10 classes overlap with our taxonomy

Why v4 Needs More Training

  • Only 1 epoch: Model barely started learning from 68K images
  • MPS limitations: Had to use conservative settings (batch=2, amp=False)
  • High loss values: Classification loss at 5.089 indicates early training stage

Recommendations

For Production: Use artifactiq:v3.0.1 - it has the best accuracy and is proven to detect domain-specific objects.

Next Steps for v4

  1. Train for 50-100 more epochs
  2. Consider cloud GPU for faster iteration (E2E Networks, JarvisLabs)
  3. v4 has larger training data (68K vs 18K) - should eventually surpass v3
