MPS-Safe Training Settings

Training on Apple Silicon's MPS backend requires specific settings to avoid tensor shape mismatch errors and memory issues:

model.train(
    data="merged_all_datasets/dataset.yaml",
    epochs=1,
    batch=2,      # Reduced from default for MPS stability
    imgsz=640,
    device="mps",
    workers=0,    # Disabled multiprocessing for MPS
    amp=False,    # MPS doesn't support FP16 properly
    cache=False,  # Large dataset - don't cache
)
Why these settings? MPS is 10-25x slower than NVIDIA GPUs and has known issues with larger batch sizes causing tensor shape mismatches. Disabling AMP and workers ensures stable training at the cost of speed.

MPS Training Challenges

Training on Apple Silicon MPS backend presents several challenges documented in our research:

Known Issues

  1. Speed: MPS is 10-25x slower than NVIDIA GPUs (RTX 4090, A100)
  2. Tensor Mismatches: Larger batch sizes cause runtime errors in loss calculation
  3. Memory Leaks: RAM usage can grow progressively during long training
  4. AMP Broken: Mixed precision training doesn't provide speedup on MPS
