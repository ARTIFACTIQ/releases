Batch Analysis: Artifactiq v2 vs Stock YOLOv8n

Published January 22, 2026 | By Artifactiq ML Team

Executive Summary

We analyzed 10 travel and fashion images using both Artifactiq v2 (custom-trained) and stock YOLOv8n (COCO-trained) models. The key finding: Artifactiq v2 detects domain-specific classes like Clothing and Shorts that stock models completely miss.

Key Result: Artifactiq v2 detected 4 apparel items (Clothing, Shorts) while Stock YOLOv8n detected 0. Stock cannot detect fashion items - it wasn't trained for that domain.

Key Findings

1. Apparel Detection: Artifactiq Exclusive

Stock YOLOv8n cannot detect clothing items because COCO dataset doesn't include fashion classes. Artifactiq v2 detected:

  • Clothing - General apparel items (1 detection)
  • Shorts - Specific apparel type (3 detections)

2. Stock Detects More Accessories (For Now)

Stock YOLOv8n detected 14 accessories (mostly backpacks) because COCO includes these classes with extensive training data. Artifactiq v2 at 18% training hasn't developed strong accessory detection yet.

3. False Positives in Stock

Stock YOLOv8n produced several questionable detections:

  • cat detected in lake trekking image (likely hikers in distance)
  • fire hydrant detected for binoculars on tripod
  • sheep detected in rocky terrain
  • bird detected on beach (no birds visible)

Conclusions

Artifactiq v2 Advantages

  • Domain-specific detection: Can detect Clothing, Shorts, and other fashion items
  • Fewer false positives: No spurious cat/sheep/fire hydrant detections
  • Fashion taxonomy: 39 classes designed for merchandising

Stock YOLOv8n Advantages

  • Higher confidence: Fully trained with 80-90% confidence on known classes
  • Better person detection: More person instances detected
  • Backpack detection: Strong accessory detection (included in COCO)

Recommendation

For fashion/travel content: Use Artifactiq - it detects apparel that stock models cannot. As training progresses (currently at 18%), confidence and detection rates will improve significantly.
